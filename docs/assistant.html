AI assistant

A chat panel (Ctrl+J) that answers from Canopus’ own data instead of guessing. It is off until you set it up.

Answers from your data

It looks at your characters, skills, current ship fit, assets, wallet and trades, computes fits with the same engine as the fitting tool, compares ships by how fast they clear a faction’s mission rooms, plans skills, checks routes for recent kills, and opens fits and sections for you.

  • For mechanics, missions and patch changes it searches a local knowledge base: the EVE University wiki and CCP’s patch notes, downloaded to your computer
  • The web only when that isn’t enough, citing its sources
  • Actions in the game client (setting a destination, saving a fit) always ask you first

Your plan or your key

It runs on OpenAI: sign in with ChatGPT (Plus or Pro — your plan pays, no separate bill) or use your own API key.

The questions you ask and the data the assistant fetches to answer them (fits, skills, prices, wallet, assets) are sent to OpenAI; your EVE login tokens and passwords never are.
