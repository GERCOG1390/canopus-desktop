Privacy

Your data stays on your computer

Canopus has no accounts, no telemetry and no server of its own. Nothing is sent to the developer.

What Canopus stores

Everything is stored on your computer: settings, saved fits, notes, game data and caches. EVE login tokens are encrypted with the system’s own protection (Windows DPAPI, the macOS Keychain or the Linux keyring) and never leave your computer.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visits as described in its privacy statement. To show the latest version and the list of changes, your browser asks GitHub’s public API about releases. The site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and its fonts are served from the site itself.
